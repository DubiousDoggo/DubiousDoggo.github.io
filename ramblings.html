10/30/2025

Its Halloweensmas eve.

Its been a long fucking week. I'm cooked. Time to relax w/ some factory game. Last I left off I had gone back to NAVIUS, after completing agricultural science pack automation on GLEBA; As well as atomating Carbon Fibre, Rocket Turret, and others. Unfortunately, the rocket turrets were not enough to stand against the Pentapods. An outpost of 2 Rocket Turret and numerous Laser Turrets was completely destoryed while defending the launch site of an artillary wagon. After contacting FULGORA, we ... ah fuck it. no time for formality, so blowing them up didn't work. The Telsa weapons from FULGORA though haven't had any issues taking them down. I returned to NAVIUS, and spent a while fixing up the factory using the new equipment from GLEBA. Biochambers are quite good! Once I had establised a (mostly) reliable supply of BioFlux shipping from GLEBA to NAVIUS, I captured some bug spawnners and began pumping them full of the stuff and harvesting the eggs. -- Turns out they make good fuel for the biochambers. -- With the biochambers fueled on bug eggs, rocket fuel production has increased by over 50%. And, as an added bonus, pollution has been decreased. Theres been some issues, the train network needed some TLC, discovered when the Bioflux train got stuck and the bug machine started making bugs again. Next on the agenda is to get a stable supply of bug eggs shipped back to GLEBA, in order to start overgrowth soil production. Then its time to prepare for AQUILLO.

11/22/25

The short lifespan of the bug eggs required the construction of a new space shuttle, GRUNGL-8, which is now in service on a dedicated route between NAVUIS and GLEBA. With the supply of eggs, overgrowth soil production has been progressing steadily. As a result, the Yumako farm has grown to a size capable of supplying Yumako at a rate faster than it can be consumed by the factory.

Conquest on NAVIUS has continued, with the northern territory expanding greatly. The supply of artillary shells ran dry during the operation, due to a lack of Tungsten being shipped from VULCANUS. A return trip to VULCANUS was required to establish a new supply of Tungsten ore. The details of this operation are currently classified, due to military involvement.

oh boy, its AQUILLO time!

Construction of a new space platform, SPANKER-3 has been completed. This vessel will be the primary transport for the AQUILLO mission, and be stationed in orbit around the planet indefinitely. Final preparations are underway, loading the platform with supplies for the mission.

The first mission was a bust. rocket production was not quick enough, and SPANKER-3 was quickly overwhelmed by the amount of large oxide asteroids. returning home, I've installed a buffer for around 2,000 rockets, and set priorities for the guns on the smaller asteroids, letting the rockets focus on the bigger ones. 11/23 I've reached AQUILLO. its cold. desolate. empty. save for myself, this sheet of ice, and an endless sea surrounding me on all sides. and so it begins anew. The factory must grow.
